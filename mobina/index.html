Mobina

A motion design piece introducing Mobina, an AI executive assistant that handles scheduling, inboxes, and busywork so people can focus on what matters.

Overview

Mobina is an AI executive assistant that manages the day-to-day busywork of running a calendar and inbox — scheduling, follow-ups, and coordination — so people can spend their time on higher-leverage work.

I directed and animated a product video for Mobina, translating what the assistant does into a short piece of motion design: clear, confident, and built to explain the product at a glance.
