Option A

Classic Card-Hand Fan

Three cards pivot from a shared point below the fan, like a hand of playing cards — ±11° angle, generous overlap. Clicking any card straightens it to 0° and brings it to the front; the previous front card swings out to fill the spot that was vacated.

Click a card, including the one furthest back.

Option 1B

Hover-to-Expand Fan

Rests in a tight cluster (like Option B), then fans out to Option A's fuller spread on hover. Clicking the right card rotates the whole set right-to-left (it becomes front, the old front swings left, the old left card wraps around to the right). Clicking the left card rotates left-to-right instead.

Hover to see it fan out, then click the left or right card to rotate the set.

Option B

Tossed Deck

A looser, more casual stack — smaller and slightly irregular rotation angles (not mirrored), like photos tossed onto a desk. Feels more candid than a formal fan.

Click a card, including the one furthest back.

Option C

Wide Radial Fan

A wider, more dramatic spread (±17°) with the two side cards slightly scaled down and faded, adding a sense of depth behind the front photo.

Click a card, including the one furthest back.

Option D

Polaroid Toss

Each photo sits inside a white polaroid-style frame with room for a caption. Warmer and more personal — pairs well with an "About" page.

Click a card, including the one furthest back.

Option E

Minimal Tight Cluster

The most restrained version — cards sit close together with a subtle ±4° tilt, mostly overlapping. Matches the site's quieter, Apple-inspired aesthetic more than a full playing-card fan would.

Click a card, including the one furthest back.
