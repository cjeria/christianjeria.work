01

Overview

Building trust at
web3 scale

Over 4.5 years at MetaMask I helped shape the experience of one of the world's most widely used web3 wallets. My work spanned onboarding, wallet interactions, and decentralised app transactions — designing for both seasoned crypto users and those brand new to the space.

I also created the now-iconic MetaMask fox logo and mascot, which has become a recognisable symbol of web3 itself.

02

The Problem

Web3 shouldn't require
a computer science degree

For most people, accessing decentralised technologies is confusing, risky, and fragmented. Seed phrases, gas fees, network switching — the mental overhead was enormous. Without the right product design, everyday users couldn't safely participate.

"Users were dropping off at every step. The onboarding wasn't a funnel — it was a wall."
🔑
03

The Solution

A gateway that feels
familiar, not foreign

MetaMask provides a secure, user-friendly wallet and gateway to the decentralised web. I simplified complex flows — key management, transactions, dapp connections — into clear, guided experiences that lowered barriers without removing control.
