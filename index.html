About

My name is Christian. I grew up in Miami, lived in San Francisco for 10 years before moving to Seattle. I'm a compulsive maker: designer, engineer, painter, and endlessly curious about how things work.

My experience in product design and years at places like Apple and MetaMask taught me that the best work happens at the intersection of craft, constraint, and human need. My approach to design is direct, intentional, and grounded in first principles. I believe in solving real problems for real people, in asking better questions before settling on answers, and in the uncompromising pursuit of clarity.

When I'm not designing or building, I'm painting, surfing, traveling, or thinking about the next thing worth making.
